### 📌 Insights from Offline Interviews

Based on interviews with professors during in-person discussions, several additional observations were noted:

Professors prefer students to provide minimal feasible experiments to demonstrate the idea's viability. These experiments often reflect the insightfulness of the student.

If the research problem is highly novel, the requirement for novelty in the method can be relatively lower.

When evaluating ideas, professors often rely on their past experience, recalling prior instances where they evaluated or were evaluated on similar ideas or problems.
